# PLEXUS experiment queue. Ordered so that if only one window ever appears, it is spent on the
# most decisive run. Resumable: a job with a marker in logs/queue/ is skipped.
#
#   ONLY_GPU=0 ./scripts/run_queue.sh scripts/queue_e0.txt &
#   ONLY_GPU=1 ./scripts/run_queue.sh scripts/queue_e0.txt &
#
# One worker per card. Claims are atomic, so the two never collide on a job.
#
# Rationale for the ordering:
#   1. The timing benchmark costs minutes and sets every schedule decision in RESEARCH_PLAN §6.
#   2. E0.1 is the reproduction gate; nothing downstream is interpretable until it passes or is
#      declared failed. Evaluating every 4 epochs makes it double as the convergence curve that
#      lever 1 needs, at ~5 extra evaluations.
#   3. ego then ego_star: the E0.2 fulcrum. If ego_star recovers star's accuracy, TAG-DLM's
#      stated explanation does not follow, which is claim C2 and does not depend on E0.1 passing.
#   4. `none` anchors the ladder; HopBias is the proposed method; random_rewire is the density
#      control and the most droppable arm (§6 lever 5).
